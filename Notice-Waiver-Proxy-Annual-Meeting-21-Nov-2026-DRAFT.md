# NOTICE OF ANNUAL MEETING OF MEMBERS

**TO:** All Members of the National Assembly entitled to vote (the Governor, the Vice Governor and the three (3) National Assemblymen of each region)

**FROM:** The Corporate Secretary / Acting Corporate Secretary, designated by Board Resolution No. ____, Series of 2026

**NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN** that the **Annual (Regular) Meeting of the Members** of The Fraternal Order of HARIBON - Haring Ibon International Inc. shall be held on the date fixed by **Section II(a) of the By-Laws** (third Saturday of November), as follows:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date** | **Saturday, 21 November 2026** |
| **Time** | ________ (a.m./p.m.) *(registration opens at ________)* |
| **Place** | ______________________________, **Makati City**, Metro Manila |

**IMPORTANT NOTE ON NOTICE.** Section II(b) of the By-Laws requires written notice at least twenty-one (21) days before the meeting. This Notice is given on a shorter period. The meeting will therefore proceed **only if every member entitled to vote signs the attached Waiver of Notice** and a quorum is present. If the waivers of all members are not received by **12:00 noon on Friday, 20 November 2026**, the meeting on 21 November 2026 will **not** proceed to election, and a new notice will be sent for the annual meeting on **Saturday, 12 December 2026**.

### AGENDA

1. Call to order
2. Proof of notice, **presentation of the signed waivers**, and certification of quorum *(a majority of the members entitled to vote, Section III of the By-Laws)*
3. Reading and approval of the minutes of the previous meeting, if any
4. Report of the National President and of the Treasurer
5. **Election of the eleven (11) members of the Board of Trustees**, for the term fixed by the By-Laws, from among the members
6. Other matters that may properly come before the meeting
7. Adjournment

### NOMINATIONS

Nominees must be members qualified under the By-Laws, the **Magna Carta** and the guidelines of the **COMELEC** of the Order. Written nominations, with the nominee's written consent, may be sent to the Corporate Secretary at ______________________ (email: ______________) **not later than 5:00 p.m. on Thursday, 19 November 2026**. Nominations may also be made from the floor, as the guidelines allow. Every member in good standing may nominate and be nominated.

### PROXIES

Members may vote **in person or by proxy** (Section IV of the By-Laws). A proxy must be in writing, signed by the member, and **received by the Corporate Secretary not later than 12:00 noon on Friday, 20 November 2026**, in the attached form. A member who cannot attend may send both the Waiver of Notice and the Proxy.

By order of the Board of Trustees.

______________________________
**Corporate Secretary / Acting Corporate Secretary**
