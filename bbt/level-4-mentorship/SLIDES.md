# Session 5 · THRIVE — Slide Specification

20 slides · 120 minutes · script: [`SCRIPT.md`](SCRIPT.md)

| # | On screen | Visual | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **THE THIRD WORD** — Buy · Borrow · **Thrive** | Cover. Third word highlighted. | A |
| 2 | **FOUR NUMBERS, ONCE A MONTH** — borrow rate · **blended** earn rate · spread · LTV | Calendar entry made in the room | B1 |

| 4 | **THE SPREAD CAN GO NEGATIVE** | One line. Sit with it. | B2 |

| 6 | **$30 BORROWED × 2% SPREAD = 60¢ / YEAR** — the carry is not the return — **the Bitcoin you never sold is the return** | Deliberately deflating. Resets expectations before they cost someone money. | B3 |
| 7 | **THE CADENCE** — daily **30 sec** · monthly **15 min** · quarterly **1 hr** · annually **half a day** | Four rhythms, increasing depth | C1 |
| 8 | **QUARTERLY** — re-plot Fib · rebalance buckets · recompute alarms · **confirm reserve is still 1:1** | The last item drifts silently as the position grows | C1 |
| 9 | **LOOK AT THE NUMBER. CLOSE THE APP.** — the failure mode is over-engagement, not neglect | The most useful behavioural slide in the session | C2 |
| 10 | **THE LOG** — date · price · LTV · borrow · earn · spread · action — *for your nerve, and for the BIR* | One line per month. Sample row shown. | C3 |
| 11 | **PERCENTAGES DON'T CHANGE** — 30% at $100 · 30% at $100,000 · 1:1 at both · 60/40 at both | Two columns, small and large, identical rules | D1 |
| 12 | **THREE THINGS DO CHANGE** — platform concentration · tax · **your own sleep** | Third one is not a joke. A tired person makes the ending decision. | D2 |
| 13 | **STOP WHEN LOSING IT WOULD CHANGE YOUR LIFE** — not annoy you. **change it.** | Write the number tonight. | D3 |
| 14 | **10 MIN BREAK** | Timer | Break |
| 15 | **BUY · BORROW · DIE** — the original mechanic — *it only completes if the asset **transfers*** | Section turn | E1 |
| 16 | **LAND TRANSFERS. BITCOIN DOESN'T.** — a title, a registry, lawyers **vs** credentials only you hold | Two columns. The core insight of the module. | E1 |
| 17 | **THRIVE** — the asset stays · capacity grows · it can fund a life without being sold | The better ending | E2 |
| 18 | **FOUR THINGS THIS QUARTER** — access document · one person who knows · **the loan is a liability your heirs inherit** · proper advice | Checkboxes. Item three surprises everybody. | E3 |
| 19 | **IF YOU DIED TONIGHT, COULD YOUR FAMILY REACH THIS?** | One question, full slide, long silence. Do not fill it. | E4 |
| 20 | **YOUR OPERATING PLAN** — position · alarms · cadence · spread rule · growth ceiling · transfer — **boring for years is the sign it's working** | Template + coach signature | Gate |
