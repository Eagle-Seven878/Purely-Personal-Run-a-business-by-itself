# LEVEL 2 & LEVEL 3 — Deep Dive and Immersion

**One curriculum. Two delivery modes.**

| | **Level 2 · Deep Dive** | **Level 3 · Immersion** |
|---|---|---|
| Price | **₱15,000** | **₱75,000** |
| Format | 3 days, live online | 3–4 whole days, face to face |
| Class size | Pods of 8, one coach each | Small class, faculty in the room |
| Modules | **1 – 6** | **1 – 8** |
| You leave with | A live position, four alarms set, your liquidation price in your own hand | All of that, **plus your complete personal playbook, built beside the faculty** |

Why one curriculum instead of two: the principle does not change with the room.
Writing an online course and a separate in-person course would mean two sets of
corrections, two sets of drift, and two standards. There is one script. Delivery
notes say what changes when the room is physical, and Modules 7 and 8 run only in
the room because they cannot be done properly any other way.

## Day shapes

**Level 2 · Online, 3 days**
- Day 1 — Module 1 (chart structure and levels)
- Day 2 — Modules 2 and 3 (platform, first execution) · *pods, one coach per eight*
- Day 3 — Modules 4, 5 and 6 (defense, scenarios, accumulation) · *the drill is gated*

**Level 3 · Immersion, 3–4 days face to face**
- Day 1 — Module 1, worked on real charts with faculty
- Day 2 — Modules 2 and 3
- Day 3 — Modules 4, 5 and 6
- Day 4 — Modules 7 and 8, plus one-to-one playbook review

## Gates

Gates are gates in both modes.

| Gate | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-flight | Before Module 2 | Both accounts verified, funds landed, apps installed. Confirmed asynchronously. **Anyone unverified rolls to the next cohort.** |
| Position Card | End of Module 3 | Live position at ≤30% LTV, liquidation price written by hand |
| Risk Card | End of Module 4 | Four alarms set on their own phone |
| **The drill** | End of Module 5 | Passes the margin-call drill **unaided, no notes**, scored individually |
| Accumulation Plan | End of Module 6 | Three buckets and a laddered deployment, coach-signed |
| Playbook | End of Module 7 *(Immersion)* | Complete personal playbook, faculty-signed |

A participant who fails the drill does not proceed to Module 6. They repeat it
with the next cohort, at no charge, with their position open and a coach
watching. That is not a penalty — Module 6 hands them a bigger position than
Module 4 taught them to defend.

## What changes face to face

The script is the same. These are the delivery differences:

- **Module 1** — real charts on a large screen, plotted by hand, faculty walking
  the room. Online this is screen-share and pod breakouts.
- **Modules 2–3** — coaches look over shoulders instead of over screen-share.
  Catching a wrong network before someone sends is meaningfully easier in a room.
- **Module 5** — the drill is delivered verbally, individually, standing up.
  Harder in person, deliberately.
- **Modules 7–8** — only in the room. A playbook built alone at a laptop is a
  form; a playbook built with someone asking *"why that number?"* is a decision.

## The closes

Both modes close, and they close into different places. Five beats, four minutes,
once — [`../_shared/ascension.md`](../_shared/ascension.md).

### Level 2 (online) → Level 3 (Immersion)

> **What you can now do.** You have a live position, defended, at 30% LTV. Four
> alarms are on your phone right now. You passed the drill without notes. Most
> people who own this asset cannot do any of that.
>
> **The gap.** Your buckets are still in your head. You have a plan for the next
> ninety days and nothing written for the next five years — no growth ceiling, no
> succession, no answer to *"what happens to this if I'm not here."* And every
> number you set this week, you set with me reading it out. Nobody has yet asked
> you **why that number and not another one.**
>
> **The next level.** Level 3, the Immersion. Three to four whole days, face to
> face, ₱75,000. You build your complete personal playbook beside the faculty,
> and you answer the transfer question properly.
>
> **Who shouldn't come.** If you're inside your first ninety days on a live
> position, don't. Run this cycle first. Come to the Immersion with a real
> drawdown behind you and you'll get five times the value from the same four
> days. Come too early and you'll build a playbook out of theory instead of
> experience. It will still be here.
>
> **One step.** Next Immersion is {{level_3_date}}. Message {{ops_lead}} with the
> date you opened your position and they'll tell you honestly if it's too soon.

### Level 3 (Immersion) → Level 4 (Done-With-You)

> **What you can now do.** You have a written playbook you built yourself:
> buckets, ladder, alarms, growth ceiling, transfer. It is yours, not ours.
>
> **The gap.** Nobody has watched you run it. A playbook survives a calm week
> easily. What you cannot know yet is what you do when it's your own money, your
> own drawdown, and nobody in the room. Alarm 3 fires about once a year. Yours
> hasn't fired yet with faculty beside you.
>
> **The next level.** Level 4, Done-With-You Mentorship. USD 3,000, with a full
> year of the Thrive Circle included. We work alongside you on your own portfolio
> decisions, through at least one real cycle.
>
> **Who shouldn't take it.** If you'd take it so someone else makes the call —
> don't. We won't, and you'd be paying us to disappoint you. And if your position
> is small enough that a year of mentorship costs more than the position itself,
> put the money in the position instead. Come back when the numbers make sense.
> We'd rather say that now than take it.
>
> **One step.** {{ops_lead}} runs a short call before anyone joins Level 4, and
> the honest answer is sometimes *"not yet."*
