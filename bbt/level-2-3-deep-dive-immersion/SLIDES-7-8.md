# MODULES 7 & 8 · PLAYBOOK AND TRANSFER — Slide Specification

17 slides · 270 minutes · **Immersion only** · script: [`MODULE-7-8-immersion.md`](MODULE-7-8-immersion.md)

A working day, not a presentation day. Slides hold the block prompt and the
faculty challenge; the participants' own paper is the real surface.

## Module 7 · Your Personal Playbook

| # | On screen | Visual | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **WHO THIS IS FOR** — you, at 3am, eighteen months from now, tired and frightened, holding a phone — *that person will not re-derive anything* | No data. Sets the whole day. | A1 |
| 2 | **THE TEST FOR EVERY LINE** — *could a tired person follow this at 3am without deciding anything?* — ✗ "manage risk appropriately" · ✓ "if LTV reaches 60%, transfer ₱____ from reserve, or repay in full" | Fail and pass side by side | **A2** |
| 3 | **BLOCK 1 · YOUR POSITION** — entry · deployed · borrowed · LTV · liquidation price · reserve — *faculty: "say your liquidation price without looking"* | Work slide, 20 min | B1 |
| 4 | **BLOCK 2 · YOUR THREE BUCKETS** — reserve · dry powder · carry — *faculty: "which account? how long to reach it? is it locked?"* | Work slide. The lock question catches most people. | B2 |
| 5 | **BLOCK 3 · YOUR LADDER** — three levels, three amounts, weighted deepest — *faculty: "price hits 0.618 tomorrow. How much, exactly?"* | Work slide | B3 |
| 6 | **BLOCK 4 · YOUR ALARMS** — four prices, set on the phone in this room — *faculty: "alarm 3 fires during a client meeting. What happens?"* | Work slide | B4 |
| 7 | **BLOCK 5 · YOUR GROWTH CEILING** — the size at which losing it would **change your life**, not annoy you — *your ceiling sits below that* — **you cannot set this honestly during a bull run** | The block nobody has considered. Faculty: *"say the number out loud."* | **B5** |
| 8 | **BLOCK 6 · YOUR NEVER-LIST** — three things, your handwriting — *faculty: "which have you already been tempted by this week?"* | Work slide | B6 |
| 9 | **READ IT OUT LOUD** — you cannot hear a vague sentence until you say it | Read-back gate. Faculty signs. | **Gate** |

## Module 8 · The Transfer Question

| # | On screen | Visual | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | **BUY · BORROW · DIE** — *the strategy only completes if the asset transfers* | Section turn | C1 |
| 11 | **LAND TRANSFERS. BITCOIN DOESN'T.** — a title, a registry, lawyers, a state that knows it exists **vs** credentials only you hold | Two columns. The core insight. | **C1** |
| 12 | **WHY WE SAY THRIVE** — the asset stays · capacity grows · it can fund a life without being sold | The better ending | C2 |
| 13 | **1 · AN ACCESS DOCUMENT** — what exists and how it is reached — **not passwords in a file** | Work slide | D1 |
| 14 | **2 · ONE PERSON WHO KNOWS** — write the name today | Work slide | D2 |
| 15 | **3 · THE LOAN IS A LIABILITY YOUR HEIRS INHERIT** — collateral **and** a loan · unserviced, it liquidates — *write it: "repay from reserve first, then hold"* | Surprises everybody. Give it room. | **D3** |
| 16 | **4 · GET PROPER ADVICE** — PH treatment of digital assets is unsettled · *doing nothing is also a decision* | Faculty states plainly this isn't theirs to give | D4 |
| 17 | **IF YOU DIED TONIGHT, COULD YOUR FAMILY REACH THIS?** | One question, full slide. **Thirty seconds of silence. Do not fill it.** | **Part 3** |

## Build checklist
- [ ] Slide 2 shows a failing line and a passing line — the test is the module
- [ ] Slide 7 present; faculty makes each person say the ceiling out loud
- [ ] Slide 9 read-back actually run — it is the gate, not a nice-to-have
- [ ] Slide 15 given room; the inherited-loan point is new to almost everyone
- [ ] Slide 17 held in silence — facilitators must not rescue the room
- [ ] Every playbook faculty-signed before anyone leaves
