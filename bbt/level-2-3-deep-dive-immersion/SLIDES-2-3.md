# MODULES 2 & 3 · THE PLATFORM AND FIRST EXECUTION — Slide Specification

34 slides · 180 minutes · script: [`MODULE-2-3-platform-execution.md`](MODULE-2-3-platform-execution.md)

Session 2 is a **demonstration session**, so the slides carry less than Session 1's
do. Most of the room's attention belongs on the shared platform screen, not on a
deck. Slides here do three jobs only: hold the checklist that must stay visible,
mark phase transitions so eight pods stay synchronised, and print the two or three
things a participant must copy down.

**Rule:** no screenshot of a platform screen goes on a slide. Screenshots go stale
between cohorts and a stale screenshot beside a live app confuses the room. Share
the live screen.
