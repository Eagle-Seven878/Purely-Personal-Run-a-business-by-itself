# TRAIN THE TRAINER — Slide Specification

11 slides · 2 days · script: [`SCRIPT.md`](SCRIPT.md)

Deliberately few slides. Day 1 is practicum, Day 2 is assessment. Slides mark
transitions and hold the rules that must stay visible while candidates work.

## Day 1 — The standard

| # | On screen | Visual | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **THE ROOM IS NOT THE CONSTRAINT** | Cover | A1 |
| 2 | **ONE FACILITATOR ≈ 2 COHORTS / MONTH** — no amount of marketing moves that number | The bottleneck, stated as arithmetic | A1 |
| 3 | **MUST NOT CHANGE / MUST CHANGE / MAY CHANGE** — disclosure, ceilings, reserve, gates, corrections, pod ratio · **the analogies** · pacing and your own examples | Three columns. The middle column is the one candidates misread as optional. | A2 |
| 4 | **NEVER A RETURN CLAIM** — not a number, not a range, not "historically", not a testimonial you repost — *automatic fail* | Full slide. Stays up during every teach-back on Day 2. | A3 |
| 5 | **THE FOUR MOVES** — typed check · calculator moment · filter · local analogy | Four panels, one line each | B1 |
| 6 | **NEVER CORRECT SOMEONE IN FRONT OF THE ROOM** — acknowledge, then add | The single mechanic new facilitators break most | B1 |
| 7 | **THE SEVEN HARD QUESTIONS** | Listed. Trainer plays the difficult participant; candidates answer cold. | B3 |
| 8 | **THE MATH, NO NOTES — 8 OF 8** | Exam slide. Eight items listed. | C1 |
| 9 | **"WE TAUGHT 50%. WE RAN THE ARITHMETIC. THE RULE IS 30% NOW. CHECK MY WORK."** | The correction, scripted. Delivered as strength, never as apology or blame. | C2 |

## Day 2 — Certification

| # | On screen | Visual | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | **TEACH-BACK RUBRIC** — accuracy 30 · mechanics 20 · timing 15 · analogy 15 · **honesty 20 (auto-fail)** | Stays on screen throughout Block D so scoring is transparent | D |
| 11 | **CERTIFIED TO RUN** — Level 0 solo · Level 1 solo · Level 2 as lead with coaches · Level 3 co-facilitated for two cohorts · **never Level 4** — **NEVER** certify others · alter the ceilings, reserve, gates or disclosure · run below 1:8 · claim a return | Two columns, permissions and prohibitions | F1 |

## Build checklist

- [ ] Maximum 12 candidates — the practicum does not work above that
- [ ] Slide 4 visible throughout every Day 2 teach-back
- [ ] Teach-back beats drawn blind, not chosen
- [ ] The three planted problems in Block E stay unannounced
- [ ] Assessment records retained; re-certification diarised annually
