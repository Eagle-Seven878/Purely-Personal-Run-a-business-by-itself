7. Trust & compliance

  • Disclosure next to every affiliate link, not only in the footer
  • Every review has a "What I don't like" section and a "Skip it if" line
  • Show the date on every review and update prices every quarter
  • List a free alternative next to every paid pick
  • Read each program's rules (some ban brand-name ads or links in email)
  • Follow the Data Privacy Act for email signups: clear consent and easy unsubscribe

8. What we never promote

  • Online casinos, betting, e-sabong, or "color game" apps
  • Crypto trading signals, forex bots, or "guaranteed return" schemes
  • MLM, recruitment-based programs, or "pay to join" ecosystems
  • Online lending or payday loan apps
  • Get-rich-quick courses or any tool that hides its pricing
