## C. OBJECTION STATEMENT (read under item 3, then ask that it be entered in the minutes)

> "Mr. Chairman, I ask that the following be entered in the minutes.
>
> Section II(a) of the By-Laws fixes the annual meeting of the members on the third Saturday of November. Section II(b) requires written notice to all members at least twenty-one (21) days before the meeting. Our Magna Carta requires a 90-day notice from COMELEC, the filing of candidacies, and the club and regional elections before the National Assembly may elect the trustees and officers. As of today, no such notice has been sent, no candidacy has been filed, and the regional elections have not been held.
>
> An election on 20, 21 or 28 November would therefore not comply with the By-Laws or the Magna Carta. I do not oppose an election. I ask that it be held properly. I propose the following steps so that we can all proceed together within the rules."

## E. IF THE OTHER TRUSTEES PROPOSE A DIFFERENT DATE

- Stay calm. Say: "I note the proposal. Please state the notice that was sent to the members and the date COMELEC issued its calendar."
- Ask for a **roll-call vote** and a recorded count. A motion that fails 5 to 6 still gets recorded.
- **The Board can't hold the election.** Only the members can elect trustees. A Board vote to hold an election on the 20th or 28th cannot make it valid.
- If they walk out, **don't adjourn.** Check that six remain and continue.
- After the meeting, send your written objection to every trustee and to the members, and tell counsel.

## F. A CONSTRUCTIVE TONE (use it in your opening and closing)

- "Nobody is being removed tonight. We are protecting the Order's records, its SEC standing and the members' right to a valid election."
