# Run-of-Show · Leading Transformational Conversations
**Nov 10 · 90 min · Facilitator: Gilbert · Mixed team**

**Room setup:** Chairs in clusters of 3 (triads). Flipchart. Timer visible. Workbooks on seats.
**Roles in every triad:** Coach · Coachee · Observer. Rotate each round.

| Clock | Min | Block |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 8 | Open and frame |
| 0:08 | 15 | 1. Activating potential |
| 0:23 | 22 | 2. Powerful questioning (practice round 1) |
| 0:45 | 3 | Reset and stretch |
| 0:48 | 20 | 3. Developing instead of taking over (practice round 2) |
| 1:08 | 14 | 4. Ownership (practice round 3) |
| 1:22 | 8 | Commitments and close |

## 0:00 · Open and Frame (8 min)
**Say:**
> "Think of the last time someone brought you a problem and you solved it in under 60 seconds. It felt helpful. It was also a small withdrawal from that person's confidence. Today is about making deposits."

**Signature story (2 min, Gilbert):** [Insert real Norte Street / Alpha chapter example: how you activated others to run cash-generating activities, and what changed.]

**Do (poll, hands up):** "Who has said 'just let me do it' this week?" Expect most hands. Normalise it.
**State the shift:** *Tell → Ask. Fix → Develop. Own it for them → Own it with them.*
**Rule:** Practice with real situations, not hypotheticals. Confidentiality in triads.

## 1 · Activating the Potential of Others (15 min)
**Input (6 min):** The Potential Gap. Most people operate at ~60% of capacity because nobody has asked what they could do. Three activators:
1. **Believe out loud.** "I think you can handle this because you did X."
2. **Raise the stretch.** Challenge one notch above comfort.
3. **Notice strengths.** Name specific behaviour, not general praise.

**Exercise (7 min) · Strengths Spotlight:** In triads, each person names one strength of a colleague (not present) with a specific example. Others add "and what could that strength make possible?"
**Debrief (2 min):** "What happened to the energy when you heard it?"

## 2 · Powerful Questioning and Coaching Conversations (22 min)
**Input (7 min):** Introduce the **GROW-lite** flow (Goal → Reality → Options → Way forward) and the question rules:
- Open, not leading ("What" and "How" over "Why" and "Don't you think...")
- Short (under 12 words)
- Then **silence**. Count to 5.

Show Weak vs Powerful (slide 2.3). Demo a 3-minute fish-bowl with a volunteer co-facilitator: Gilbert first *tells* (bad), then *asks* (good). Ask the room what changed.

**Practice round 1 (12 min):** 4 min per rotation. Coachee brings a real, low-stakes work challenge. Coach may only ask questions, no advice. Observer tallies questions vs statements and notes the best question.
**Debrief (3 min):** "Which question moved the conversation most?" Capture 3 on the flipchart.

## Reset (3 min)
Stand, stretch, swap triad members.

## 3 · Developing People Instead of Taking Over (20 min)
**Input (6 min):** The **Takeover Trap**. Why we do it (speed, perfectionism, being needed), what it costs (dependence, burnout, disengagement). The **Delegation Ladder**: Do it for them → Show then do together → They do, I review → They do, they report → They own it.
**Self-check (3 min, workbook):** Mark where you sit on the ladder for 3 recurring tasks.

**Practice round 2 (9 min):** 3 min per rotation. Coachee presents a moment they took over. Coach asks: "What would it take for them to handle this next time?" and "What support do they need from you, specifically?" Observer notes any slips into advice.
**Debrief (2 min):** "What does it feel like to *not* rescue?"

## 4 · Empowering Team Members to Take Ownership (14 min)
**Input (5 min):** Ownership = **Clarity + Authority + Accountability + Support**. If any one is missing, it is not ownership, it is just delegated work. Introduce the **Hand-back Script**:
> "This one is yours. Here's the outcome I need and by when. You decide how. What do you need from me, and when should we check in?"

**Practice round 3 (7 min):** Each person drafts a Hand-back Script for a real task in the workbook, then delivers it to their triad. Observer checks all four elements.
**Debrief (2 min):** One volunteer shares the script they will actually use.

## Commitments and Close (8 min)
1. **Three commitments (workbook, 3 min):** One conversation I'll hold this week, one task I'll hand back, one habit (e.g., "count to 5").
2. **Pair share (2 min):** Say commitment aloud to a partner, who confirms a check-in date.
3. **Close (3 min).**
> "Transformation does not happen in the big speech. It happens in the next conversation. Ask one more question than you normally would."

Send the workbook photo and the 3-question cheat card within 24 hours.

---

## Facilitator Notes
- **If the room goes quiet in triads:** Walk over and give the Coach one starter question from the workbook bank.
- **If someone says "this takes too long":** Reply, "Faster this week, slower for you next quarter. Which would you rather?"
- **If time runs short:** Cut the fish-bowl demo to 2 min and merge Block 4's input into the practice round. Never cut the practice rounds.
- **Avoid:** Asking people to share personal stories in plenary. Keep the vulnerable stuff in triads.
