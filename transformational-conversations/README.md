# Leading Transformational Conversations
### November 10 · 90 minutes · Mixed team · Facilitator: Gilbert

**Outcome:** Every participant leaves able to turn a "just tell me what to do" moment into a coaching conversation, with one real conversation scheduled and one real task handed back for ownership.

## Four Topics
1. Activating the Potential of Others
2. Powerful Questioning & Coaching Conversations
3. Developing People Instead of Taking Over
4. Empowering Team Members to Take Ownership

## Design Principles
- **One shift:** from *telling* to *asking*. Everything else supports it.
- **70% practice, 30% input.** Nobody learns coaching by listening about coaching.
- **Mixed team = mixed levels.** "Leader" means anyone who influences someone else's work, so exercises use peer triads, not manager/report pairs.
- **Rule of 3.** Max 3 frameworks, 3 practice rounds, 3 commitments.
