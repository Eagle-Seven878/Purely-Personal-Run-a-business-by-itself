## 2 · Powerful Questioning
**GROW-lite:** Goal → Reality → Options → Way forward

**Question bank**
- *Goal:* What would you like to be different? · What does success look like?
- *Reality:* What's happening now? · What have you tried so far? · What's getting in the way?
- *Options:* What else could you do? · What would you do if you knew you couldn't fail? · Who could help?
- *Way forward:* What will you do first? · By when? · What support do you need?
- *Deepeners:* Tell me more. · What else? · What's the real challenge here?

**Rules:** Open · Short (under 12 words) · Count to 5 after asking.
