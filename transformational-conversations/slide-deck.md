## Block 2 · Powerful Questioning
**[2.1] Why questions beat answers.** People support what they create.
**[2.2] GROW-lite.** Goal → Reality → Options → Way forward (4 colored blocks)
**[2.3] Weak vs Powerful.** Two-column:
- "Don't you think you should...?" → "What options do you see?"
- "Why didn't you...?" → "What got in the way?"
- "Have you tried X?" → "What have you tried so far?"
- "Is that clear?" → "What's your plan from here?"
**[2.4] The 5-second silence.** Visual countdown. "The next idea is usually the best one."
**[2.5] Practice round 1.** Roles: Coach · Coachee · Observer. Coach: questions only. (12 min)
**[2.6] What did you notice?** Debrief prompts.

## Block 3 · Developing, Not Taking Over
**[3.1] The Takeover Trap.** Why we do it: speed, perfectionism, being needed.
**[3.2] The cost.** Dependence · Burnout · Disengagement
**[3.3] The Delegation Ladder.** 5 rungs, Do for them → They own it
**[3.4] Where are you on the ladder?** Workbook self-check
**[3.5] Two rescue-stoppers.** "What would it take for you to handle this?" · "What support do you need from me, specifically?"
**[3.6] Practice round 2.** (9 min)

## Block 4 · Ownership
**[4.1] Ownership formula.** Clarity + Authority + Accountability + Support
**[4.2] Missing a piece?** Four failure patterns: Unclear outcome · No authority · No follow-through · Left alone
**[4.3] The Hand-back Script.** "This one is yours. Here's the outcome and by when. You decide how. What do you need from me, and when do we check in?"
**[4.4] Practice round 3.** Draft, deliver, observer checks all four elements. (7 min)
