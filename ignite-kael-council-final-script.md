# Ignite Kael Council: Final Accountability Meeting (15 min)

Ignite Batch 1 graduates tomorrow, Council included. Fill in the [brackets] before you start.

## 1. Cover (0:00 to 2:00)
Good [morning/afternoon], Council. This is our last accountability meeting. Tomorrow Ignite Batch 1 graduates, and that includes every one of us on this Council. Thank you, and thank you, Coach. Today's agenda is one word: commitment. In the next fifteen minutes you will write it, say it, and take it with you.

## 2. Why we are here (2:00 to 4:00)
A commitment that no one reviews is only an intention. The Council exists so that intentions become results. Graduation is not the finish line. It is the day accountability moves from us to you.
Today we do three things:
1. You write three commitments: to yourself in three months, to your family in six months, to your company or community in one year.
2. You read them aloud, because a promise said in public is harder to break.
3. You take them with you as a gift, saved on your phone, with reminders on the dates they fall due.

## 3. Write your three commitments (4:00 to 11:00)
Take three minutes to write, in silence. One sentence each, plus the proof that shows you did it.
- **3 months, to myself:** a commitment on your skills. What will you learn or master, and how will we see it?
- **6 months, to my family:** what will you do for the people who carry you?
- **1 year, to my company or community:** what will you deliver?

[Write for 3 minutes.]
Now we share. Each person reads their three commitments aloud, in this order: myself, my family, my company. Thirty seconds each. No one comments until everyone has read. For a large batch, take three or four volunteers and let the rest post in the group chat.

## 4. Your gift from Coach (11:00 to 13:00)
This is my gift to you: a commitment card. Phones out.
1. Open the card at [link] and type your three commitments.
2. Tap **Save card to my phone**.
3. Tap **Copy for group chat** and paste it into the batch group.
4. Tap the three **Add** buttons so your calendar reminds you at 3 months, 6 months and 1 year.
When the reminder comes, open the card, read what you promised, and ask: did I do it?

## 5. Carry it forward (13:00 to 15:00)
Three commitments, at three months, six months and one year, and [name] will check in with you on [date]. Is there any commitment you want to challenge before we finish? [Pause.] Thank you, Council, for your time and your honesty. Thank you, Coach, for holding us to a higher standard. Tomorrow Ignite Batch 1 graduates, Council included, and today we make these real.
