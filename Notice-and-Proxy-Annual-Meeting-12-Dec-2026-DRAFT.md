This is the annual meeting of the members for the year 2026. It is set for the date above so that every member receives the **full twenty-one (21) days' written notice** required by Section II(b) of the By-Laws.

### AGENDA

1. Call to order
2. Proof of notice and certification of quorum *(a majority of the members entitled to vote, Section III of the By-Laws)*
3. Reading and approval of the minutes of the previous meeting, if any
4. Report of the National President and of the Treasurer
5. **Election of the eleven (11) members of the Board of Trustees**, for the term fixed by the By-Laws, from among the members
6. [**Proposal to remove the following trustees: ______________________________, which requires the affirmative vote of at least two-thirds (2/3) of the members entitled to vote.** *(Include this item only if the removal is to be proposed. 7. Other matters that may properly come before the meeting
8. Adjournment

### NOMINATIONS

Nominees for trustee must be members qualified under the By-Laws, the **Magna Carta** and the guidelines of the **COMELEC** of the Order. Written nominations, with the nominee's written consent, may be submitted to the Corporate Secretary at ______________________ (email: ______________) **not later than 5:00 p.m. on Saturday, 5 December 2026**. Nominations may also be made from the floor, as the guidelines allow. Every member in good standing has the right to nominate and be nominated.

### PROXIES

Members may vote **in person or by proxy** (Section IV of the By-Laws). A proxy must be **in writing, signed by the member, and received by the Corporate Secretary not later than 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, 9 December 2026**, in the attached form or any other form authorized by the By-Laws. A proxy is valid only for this meeting unless it states otherwise and in no case for longer than five (5) years.

### BOARD MEETING NOTE

At the organizational meeting of the newly elected Board, held **immediately after the election** (Section VI of the By-Laws), the trustees shall elect the President, Treasurer, Secretary and other officers of the Order.

By order of the Board of Trustees.
