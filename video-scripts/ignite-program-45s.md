# IGNITE Program: for coaches who post and get no clients

**Runtime:** ~40–45 seconds · 139 words
**Platform:** Instagram Reels / TikTok / Shorts (vertical, talking to camera)
**Voice:** Daniel Paul's Voice DNA (from `ai-employee-bootcamp/shared/voice-dna.md`)
**Source:** Brain dump from repo (Voice DNA and ICP only). No IGNITE details were provided, so none are invented.

## THE SCRIPT

### Hook

*(straight to camera, relaxed, like you're calling out one person)*

You're a coach, you've been posting for months, you get the likes, and nobody is booking a call.

This video is for you.

---

> ### ↕ Swap-in hooks — not part of the script, skip past to keep filming
>
> **A.** If you're a consultant who's good at the work but invisible online, this one's for you. — better if your audience skews consultants and founders, not coaches.
>
> **B.** Your posts get engagement and zero leads, and I want to tell you why. — better if you want the problem named first and the audience left open.

---

### Body

Because that's usually not a content problem, and posting more won't fix it.

*(small shrug, then lean in)*

Most people in your spot post more, and they get more likes, and the inbox stays just as empty, because the right people can't tell what you actually do when they land on your profile.

*(pause)*

You're good at what you do. You already know that. It's just that nobody online can see it yet.

That's exactly what **IGNITE** is for. It's built for coaches, consultants, and founders who are great at the work and invisible online, so the right people find you, and they actually reach out.

### Close

*(point down toward the comments)*

If that's you, comment **IGNITE** and I'll send you the details.

---

## Why this hook

It names the exact situation (posting, getting likes, no calls) and says outright who it's for. A stranger in that spot knows in one second it's about them. A cleverer line would hide that.
