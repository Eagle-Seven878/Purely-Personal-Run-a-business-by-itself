> **Pillars:** Business Clarity · Cash Flow · Entrepreneurship · Leadership · Systems · AI & Business Growth
> **Format:** vertical 9:16, 55–75 seconds. Film one line per take.
> **Platforms:** Facebook/Instagram Reels · TikTok · YouTube Shorts · LinkedIn (English versions work best on LinkedIn)

## How to pick your option

For every script, choose:

1. **One hook** (A, B or C). **A** is the recommended one. Use B or C to test a second version later.
2. **One language**:
   - **English** for LinkedIn and international viewers.
   - **Taglish** for Facebook, TikTok and Filipino viewers. It usually feels more personal to a local audience.
3. **Post the same script twice.** Try Taglish on Facebook/TikTok and English on LinkedIn. Compare the numbers after 72 hours.

**Why these versions are the strongest:**
- **Fast start:** every hook works in the first 3 seconds and names a specific pain, number or scene.
- **Research-backed hooks:** each opens a small, specific curiosity gap, which the research shows works better than a vague one.
- **Held attention:** there's a turn in the middle ("The thing is," / "And here is the part most people miss") so viewers keep watching.
- **One of each:** one scene, one lesson, one ask.
- **Real proof:** Gilbert's numbers come only from his documented proof points. Daniel's use numbers as instructions, not invented results.
- **Accuracy fixes:** G5 no longer guesses the coach's gender. D5's personal claim is now a neutral line.

**Shot notes** are in *italics* inside brackets. Don't read them out loud.

# PART 1 · COACH GILBERT (NOVAREIGN)

**Voice rules:**
- Scene first, principle last.
- Build with long "and... so that..." sentences, then land on a short verdict.
- Exact ₱ figures and real places.
- Talk to one reader, like a brother.
- Close on a declaration, never a question.

**Never:** hype, "act now", "limited spots", "hustle harder", "Here's the thing."

**Signature close:** *"If you're earning but you can't name your breakeven, that is the first number we find. Message me and we start there."*

# PART 2 · DANIEL PAUL (PURELY PERSONAL)

**Voice rules:**
- 2-line hook: a declaration, then a line starting "Because..." or "Then,..."
- The next line starts with "Yeah." or "So,".
- Pivot with "The thing is," and give the instruction with "Here's what I want you to do."
- One triple-fragment block, one short landing line, at least 2 specific numbers, exact tool names.
- End with a keyword CTA and a PS question.

**Never:** em dashes, unlock, leverage, elevate, game-changer, seamless.

**Taglish versions:** the English signature markers ("Yeah.", "The thing is,", "Here's what I want you to do.") stay in English on purpose, the way Taglish speakers mix naturally. The CTA keywords stay in English. Money figures are in ₱ for a Filipino audience.
