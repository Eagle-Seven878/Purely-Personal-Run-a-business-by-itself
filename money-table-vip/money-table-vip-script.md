# The Money Table VIP Package: Presenter Script
Philippine Cashflow Club · 61 slides · about 85 to 100 minutes with Q&A

Open `money-table-vip.html` in Chrome, full screen. Arrow keys move, **N** shows this script on the slide, **S** opens speaker view.

Numbers follow the Club's latest membership journey graphic (reference values): VIP benefit value P305,000 + Gold additional P280,000 + Platinum training P170,000 = P755,000 total journey reference value. VIP + Gold = P585,000. VIP enrollment 2026: P60,000. Planned 2027 price: P75,000. Opportunity pathways are by invitation, with no monetary value.

**Say this clearly (it is on the Club's own graphic):** these are Club-assigned reference values, not prices. Gold and Platinum are progression levels you grow into. What the buyer enrolls in is VIP at P60,000.

## Pre-flight checklist: confirm before you go live

Each item is marked «CONFIRM» or «PRIVATE» in the script below. None appear on the slides.

- [ ] Exact cut-off date for the 2026 rate (for example December 31, 2026).
- [ ] Exact Platinum qualification: what counts as a real-life deal.
- [ ] SRT (Sir Ron's Table) Games and RELM are qualifications the Club observes. Never explain them. If asked: "You will discover why as you play."
- [ ] GRADUATION PACE: it depends on the participant, and games now run daily. Confirm the daily schedule is live before saying so. Also decide whether SRT and RELM can run alongside the 10 games, and set a limit on "play more" loops.
- [ ] Your personal reason for rewarding early members.
- [ ] Regular Family Member price, if you have one.
- [ ] Membership validity and renewal terms (awaiting Board approval in the manual).
- [ ] PAYMENT (proposed, not yet approved): 20,000 down then two equal payments of 20,000; whether the 2026 rate locks on first verified payment; accepted methods (GCash, Maya, bank, cash); official account details; down-payment refund terms.
- [ ] Guarantee: say none unless the refund policy is approved.
- [ ] Real member testimonials, word for word, with permission.
- [ ] Photos are from past Money Table events. Confirm attendees are comfortable with their faces on your slides, and swap any you prefer not to show.
- [ ] Registration link (so the QR code shows), start date and venue or Zoom.
- [ ] Any extra bonus for people who join in the room.
