# Podcast Script: "When the Rules Change"

**Show:** The Money Table Podcast · Philippine Cashflow Club
**Episode:** Inside The Money Table VIP Package
**Length:** about 14 minutes · **Format:** HOST plus one CO-HOST (a facilitator or member)
**Tone:** warm, curious, plain English, a little Taglish is welcome.

> Items marked «CONFIRM» need your real answer before you record. Nothing in this script invents a testimonial, a date or a guarantee.

## Episode title and description

**Title:** When the Rules Change, Can You Change? The Money Table VIP
**Description:** What happens when the Big Deal cards are removed and you must adapt? We walk through the Philippine Cashflow Club journey from VIP to Gold to Platinum, what it includes, and how the 2026 enrollment works.

## 02:00 · The moment the rules change

**HOST:** Let me tell you what we see, game after game. A player sits down. Small deals, build capital, wait for the Big Deal, get out of the Rat Race. Feels great.

**CO-HOST:** And then?

**HOST:** And then life does not keep the rules the same. In our Graduation Game, the Big Deal cards are removed. The player has to discover another way.

**CO-HOST:** What do people feel in that moment?

**HOST:** Frustration. Sometimes fear. Some say, "This is not fair." And that moment is exactly where they grow.

**CO-HOST:** And our facilitators do not give the answer, right?

**HOST:** Right. We guide with questions. The player discovers it themselves. That is the rule: guide, do not give away.

## 04:30 · The journey

**HOST:** So let's map the journey. It starts with VIP. Think of VIP as your starting point: learn and practise.

**CO-HOST:** Then Gold.

**HOST:** Gold is "beyond VIP": develop and expand. After the VIP Game, you move into unlimited club games, advanced games, and further training.

**CO-HOST:** And Platinum?

**HOST:** Platinum is the highest membership level. Any VIP can qualify, but it asks for more: a real-life deal, not just a game deal. Gold is about deals in the game. Platinum is about a real-life deal.

**CO-HOST:** And opportunities come with it?

**HOST:** Opportunity pathways, yes, by invitation. Speaking, facilitation, training, coaching, chapter creation, travel, and at Platinum, leadership and business partnerships. They depend on qualifications and availability, so we do not put a money value on them.

## 07:30 · What VIP includes

**HOST:** Let me give you the picture without reading a price list.

**CO-HOST:** Please.

**HOST:** In VIP you have your 10 Regular Games, your SRT games, RELM, the Graduation Game, the VIP Game, and VIP training: Irresistible Offer, Facilitation, Teach to Be Rich, and Masterpiece of a Deal.

**CO-HOST:** What is SRT?

**HOST:** That is Sir Ron's Table. I will keep it as a surprise: you will discover why as you play. Same with RELM.

**CO-HOST:** And in order?

**HOST:** SRT is separate from the 10 games, and we recommend doing it before them. RELM is completed before your assessment. And the assessment can happen as early as before your 10th game, once you have the basics. Then comes Graduation.

«CONFIRM: the assessment timing and that the daily schedule is live before you say this on air.»

**CO-HOST:** So how long does it take?

**HOST:** It depends on you, on how soon you want the basics. Our games now run daily, so the more you play, the sooner you graduate.
