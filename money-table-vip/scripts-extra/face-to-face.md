# The Irresistible Offer: Face-to-Face Presentation Script

**For:** a live, in-person session · **Offer:** The Money Table VIP Package · **Length:** 20 to 25 minutes, offer only (no teaching)
**Use this after your teaching or a game session.** It is the offer and the close, ready to deliver standing in the room.

> «CONFIRM» items are decisions only you can make. Nothing here invents a testimonial, a date, a seat count or a guarantee.

## Before you go on: room checklist

- [ ] Printed **journey cards** (one per seat): the membership journey graphic with VIP, Gold and Platinum.
- [ ] **Offer sheets** or order forms (name, mobile, email, membership type, payment method, amount, reference number).
- [ ] **Pens**, a receipt book or tablet for the official receipt.
- [ ] Official **payment details**: GCash or Maya QR, bank details, cash handled only by named team members. Never use personal accounts «CONFIRM».
- [ ] A **team of closers**, one per two or three tables, each wearing the Club badge and standing at the back.
- [ ] A **welcome table** with a sign that reads VIP, plus someone to take a group photo of new members.
- [ ] Screen with the price slide ready (P60,000) and the journey graphic.
- [ ] «CONFIRM» the 2026 cut-off date, the payment structure, and any room-only bonus.

## The flow at a glance

1. Permission
2. The name, as the answer
3. Where and when
4. Everything you get, tier by tier
5. The anchor
6. The reason
7. The price
8. The limit
9. How to pay
10. Your seat is documented
11. The close and the walk to the table
12. If someone hesitates
13. Welcome

## 1 · Permission (1 min)

[Stand still. Look at the whole room. Smile.]

**SAY:** "Thank you for playing with us today. Earlier I told you there would be an offer. So I am asking permission. Would it be okay if I shared a small offer with you? No pressure. It takes a few minutes. If it is okay, raise your hand."

[Wait. Look for hands. Acknowledge them: "Thank you. I see you."]

## 2 · The name, as the answer (2 min)

**SAY:** "How many of you want to know exactly what to do next, after your first games? Raise your hand if that is you."

[Pause. Count hands out loud.]

**SAY:** "That is why the Philippine Cashflow Club created **The Money Table VIP Package**. Learn. Practise. Develop. Lead."

**SAY:** "The most important thing you get is your complete membership journey. You start at VIP. You grow into Gold. And Platinum, the highest level, is for those who prove it with a real-life deal."

## 3 · Where and when (1 min)

**SAY:** "You start on the day your payment is verified. That is your Official Joining Date. You begin with an orientation, by Zoom or face to face. Every game you play is verified and tracked in your record. Our games now run daily, so your pace is yours."

«CONFIRM: say "games run daily" only if the daily schedule is live. Add the real venue, schedule and start date if you have them.»

## 4 · Everything you get (6 min)

[Hold up the journey card. Walk slowly between the tables as you speak.]

**SAY:** "Look at your journey card. Let me walk you through it."

**VIP: your starting point.**

**SAY:** "First, your 10 Regular Games, valued by the Club at 15,000 pesos. Then 3 SRT Games, 15,000. We recommend doing them before your 10 games. Then RELM, 15,000, completed before your assessment. Then the Graduation Game, where the rules change, 25,000. Then the VIP Game, 25,000. And VIP Training: Irresistible Offer, Facilitation, Teach to Be Rich, and Masterpiece of a Deal, together 210,000."

[Pause.]

**SAY:** "That is the VIP benefit value: 305,000 pesos."

«PRIVATE: do not explain what SRT or RELM are for. If asked: "You will discover why as you play." The assessment can happen as early as before the 10th game and covers SRT and RELM. Do not say what it covers.»

**Gold: beyond VIP.**

**SAY:** "After the VIP Game, you grow into Gold. Unlimited Club Games. The value is based on 50 games at 1,500 pesos, but it is not a limit. You can play unlimited. Then Advanced Games, 30,000. Then Gold Training: Public Speaking, Training and Coaching Development, and Leadership, together 175,000. Gold adds 280,000 pesos."

**Platinum and opportunities.**

**SAY:** "And Platinum, the highest level: Psychology of Winning and Irresistible Confidence, 170,000 pesos of training. Any VIP can qualify, but it asks for more: a real-life deal, not just a game deal."

**SAY:** "And beginning at Gold, opportunity pathways open by invitation: speaking, facilitation, training, coaching, chapter creation, and travel. At Platinum, advanced leadership and business partnerships. We put no money value on these, because they depend on qualifications and availability."

## 5 · The anchor (1 min)

**SAY:** "Add it up. The total journey reference value is 755,000 pesos."

[Stop talking. Silence. Three seconds.]

**SAY:** "Please hear this clearly. These are Club-assigned reference values. They are not prices. Gold and Platinum are separate progression levels. You grow into them. What you enroll in today is VIP."

## 6 · The reason (1 min)

**SAY:** "Why am I offering this at a special rate? Because the planned price for 2027 is 75,000 pesos. If you enroll in 2026, you keep the 2026 rate."

«CONFIRM: add your personal reason, in your own words, for rewarding early members. A true story is stronger than any line here.»

## 7 · The price (2 min)

[STAR MOMENT. Say nothing for three seconds. Let them wait.]

**SAY:** "Not 755,000 pesos. VIP enrollment in 2026 is **60,000 pesos**."

[Hold the silence. Do not fill it.]

**SAY:** "The planned 2027 price is 75,000. So you save 15,000. VIP alone carries 305,000 pesos of Club reference value, about five times what you enroll for."

## 8 · The limit (30 sec)

**SAY:** "The 2026 rate ends with the year. After that, the planned price is 75,000."

«CONFIRM: the exact cut-off date. Say it out loud. Never invent a seat limit or a deadline that is not real.»

## 9 · How to pay (2 min)

**SAY:** "There are two simple ways to join. Option one: pay in full, 60,000 pesos. Your 2026 rate is locked when your payment is verified. Option two: reserve your seat with a down payment of 20,000 pesos, and complete the balance in two equal payments."

**SAY:** "You can pay by GCash, Maya, bank transfer, or cash, with one of our team members at the back. One rule: pay only to the official Club account, or to a team member in a Club badge. Never to a personal account you have not verified."

«CONFIRM, proposed and not yet approved: the installment amounts and balance deadline, whether the 2026 rate locks when the first payment is verified, the accepted methods, and the down-payment refund terms.»

## 10 · Your seat is documented (30 sec)

**SAY:** "Your seat is documented: an official receipt, a Member ID, and a signed agreement. Your path is on record."

«CONFIRM: the refund, transfer and cancellation policy is awaiting Board approval. Do not say "guarantee" or promise refunds until it is approved.»

## 11 · The close and the walk to the table (3 min)

**SAY:** "Bayanihan. Kapwa. Katiwala. Together, as one community, caring for each other, and being good stewards. Your place is ready."

**SAY:** "Here is exactly what to do. Step one: if you want VIP, stand up. Step two: walk to the back, where our team is waiting. Step three: choose how you will pay, and they will take care of you. If you need a few minutes, a team member will sit with you."

[Say nothing. Stay in the room. Look at the back. Smile.]

**SAY:** "Standing up is not a payment. It is a decision. I will wait."

[Count aloud, slowly, as people stand: "I see one. Thank you. Two. Welcome."]

## 12 · If someone hesitates (for you or your team)

- **"I need to think about it."** — "Of course. What part would you like to think about: the pace, the price, or whether it is right for you? Let us talk it through now, so you decide with all the facts."
- **"It is a lot of money."** — "I understand. That is why there is a reserve option: 20,000 now locks the 2026 rate, and the balance follows in two payments." «CONFIRM the structure first.»
- **"When do I graduate?"** — "It depends on you, on how soon you want the basics. Our games run daily, so the more you play, the sooner you graduate. Your facilitator will show you exactly where you are." «Never promise a date.»
- **"Do I have to do SRT or RELM?"** — "SRT is separate from your 10 games, and we recommend doing it before them. RELM is completed before your assessment. You will discover why as you play."
- **"Will I get Gold and Platinum?"** — "Gold comes after the VIP Game as you progress. Platinum is the highest level, and it asks for a real-life deal. They are progression levels you grow into, not purchases."
- **"Is there a refund?"** — "Your facilitator will explain our policy." «CONFIRM the policy before this day.»

## 13 · Welcome (2 min)

[Applause for each person who joins. Photograph the group at the welcome table.]

**SAY:** "Welcome to the family. Your team will give you your receipt, your Member ID and your orientation details. For those who are still thinking, thank you for playing. Your facilitator will follow up, and you still keep the lesson from today: when the rules change, you can change."

[Smile. Stay for photos and conversations.]

## After the session

- Same day: send each new member their receipt, Member ID, and orientation link.
- Within 24 hours: message everyone who stood up but did not finish, with a warm check-in. «CONFIRM the follow-up message wording.»
- Keep a simple list: name, option chosen, amount, verified yes or no.
