What we see every week

Big revenue. No idea of the net.

Founders announce ₱1.2 billion in gross sales and cannot tell you their net income percentage. Then they ask where to borrow ₱100 million. Business owners borrow at 5% a month because they want to, not because the numbers said so. Owners grind for years without knowing why, for how long, or for how much.

Gilbert headed the accounting of 20+ companies. When a lending client asked to borrow ₱5 to ₱10 million at 5% a month, he asked to see their financial statements first and talked them down to a lower rate. They opened their books to him and invited him onto their board.

Clarity comes first. Then options. Then execution.

How Coach Gilbert teaches it

Three rules he lives by

Breakeven first

Before any campaign, find the breakeven quantity and make it a KPI. From it you see your real strength, and you decide whether to keep a product or cancel it.

Let the speed of breakeven set the speed of scaling

If you clear breakeven fast, scale faster and bigger. If it takes a long time, scale slowly and in smaller volume. Not appetite. Not ego. The number.

Know your survival line

Breakeven is the level where you can say “we are surviving.” Know how many months your cash can hold you there, and you can plan the next six months on purpose.
