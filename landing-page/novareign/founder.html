Our founder

Gilbert H. Trani

Founder & CEO of NOVAREIGN Global Health Ventures Inc. Finance officer, pharmaceutical operator, mentor and civic leader. Friends and students call him Coach Gilbert.

“What kind of Philippines are we building if this product reaches a million people?”

The question every NOVAREIGN formula has to answer

The road here

He came up the hard way, and it shaped every product.

Childhood

The fish landing before sunrise

As a boy he helped unload iced fish at the landing and was paid in one or two small fish. He chopped kangkong for pig farmers and sold plastic bags to fish buyers for a few cents. He found income in every corner, because at home there was no other way.

College

A ₱200 allowance that never moved

He went to college in Tacloban on a government scholarship. His allowance was ₱200 and it never increased. So he went back to the fish landing, held the fish he was given until daylight, and sold them to buy food. He slept in a loft, ate noodles meal after meal, became a student leader, and stayed in school.

Drug Makers Laboratories

Five promotions in two years

He applied for accounting clerk and was hired as a collector. He treated every extra task as training with a salary, and learned costing, payables, receivables and letters of credit. He rose to Finance Officer, headed the accounting of all the companies in the group, and argued the company's cases before the Bureau of Customs. The financial statements of 20+ companies crossed his desk every month.

The hardest season

Four kilometres through the mud

When his corporate career ended, he went to the farm. He walked four kilometres through mud to pick guava, pineapple and bananas, and sold them along the road home. Later he drove 18 to 20 hours a day for Grab and Uber. He fell many times. He kept going.

Nueva Vizcaya

The ₱500,000 purchase order

Seven hours from home, researching the black pepper trade, he saw a request for quotation from a provincial government and answered it. He hoped for ₱100,000. The purchase order was ₱500,000, and it paid more than ₱200,000 in income without using his own capital, because a supplier he had once helped gave him credit. That was the birth of ARB Pharmaceutical.

Today

NOVAREIGN

Everything he learned about sourcing, compliance and numbers now goes into NOVAREIGN: functional products for Filipinos, a home for aligned partner brands, and mentorship for business owners who want to see their real numbers.

How he does business

Give the seed. Watch what it becomes.

At Drug Makers, a small distributor asked for a ₱10,000 credit line to keep his business rolling. Gilbert gave it and expected nothing back.

That distributor is now one of the biggest pharmaceutical wholesalers in the country. Years later, the relationship returned as a ₱10 million credit line on 30- and 60-day terms, which funds Gilbert's return to pharmaceuticals.

That is the NOVAREIGN way: not what will I get from you, but what we can do together.

Service

Two boiled eggs

With the Fraternal Order of Eagles, Gilbert led humanitarian missions to calamity sites across the country. In Cagayan, crossing floodwaters to deliver relief, an old woman found him, hugged him and cried as she thanked him for two boiled eggs.

He broke down. Two eggs is not supposed to matter. To her it was everything, the same way a handful of kangkong and a few coins were once everything to him. He has not been the same since.
