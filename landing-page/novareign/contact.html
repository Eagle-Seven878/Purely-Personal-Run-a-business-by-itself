Questions

          
What people ask us first

        

        

          

            How does the 30-day guarantee work?
            
Use the product as directed for thirty days. If you do not feel or see a real difference, message us within that window and we will return your money. No questions asked.

          

          

            I have tried many brands and nothing worked. Why would this be different?
            
You are right to be careful. We name our ingredient sources, work only with licensed manufacturers, and revise formulas until the founder would stake his name on the result. And if it still does not work for you, the guarantee means you lose nothing.

          

          

            Why don't you lower your prices?
            
Because the price is what it costs to make a product that works. Competing on the lowest price is how ingredients get cut. We would rather give you a guarantee than a discount.

          

          

            Is NOVAREIGN a network marketing company?
            
No. There is no recruitment ladder. Distributors sell products to customers, and partner brands run their own businesses with NOVAREIGN as their operating backbone.

          

          

            Where do you deliver?
            
We are based in Makati City and serve Metro Manila first, with nationwide delivery as our online store opens. Tell us your location in your message and we will confirm.

          

          

            Who is the financial clarity mentorship for?
            
Filipino SME owners and coaches who are already earning but do not know their breakeven, net income percentage or survival months. If you only need a quick answer, Coach Gilbert will tell you, and he may simply compute your breakeven with you.
