# BOARD RESOLUTION NO. ____, SERIES OF 2026

**A RESOLUTION DESIGNATING A SECRETARY PRO TEMPORE TO ISSUE THE NOTICE, RECEIVE THE WAIVERS AND PROXIES, AND KEEP THE RECORDS OF THE 2026 ANNUAL MEETING OF THE MEMBERS**

At a special meeting of the Board of Trustees (the "Board") of The Fraternal Order of HARIBON - Haring Ibon International Inc. (the "Order") held on ______ November 2026 at ______, called by the President under Section I(b) of the By-Laws, with notice to all trustees given at least two (2) days before the meeting or waived (Section I(c)), a **quorum of at least six (6) of the eleven (11) trustees** being present, the Board adopted the following:

**WHEREAS**, under **Section VI of the By-Laws**, the Board elects the officers of the Order, including a **Secretary who must be a citizen and resident of the Philippines**, and may fill an office when the need arises;

**WHEREAS**, the annual meeting of the members, under **Section II of the By-Laws**, requires written notice to every member, receipt of proxies by the Corporate Secretary, proof of notice, a certified quorum, and proper minutes and records;

**WHEREAS**, the person now acting as Corporate Secretary has not been attending meetings, no complete minutes and records have been kept, and the Board must ensure that the notices, waivers, proxies and records required for the annual meeting are issued and kept **on time and in accordance with the By-Laws**;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, on motion duly made and seconded, the Board hereby **RESOLVES**, as it is hereby **RESOLVED**:

**1. RESOLVED**, that **______________________________**, a citizen and resident of the Philippines and a **[member / trustee]** of the Order, is **designated Secretary pro tempore** of the Order, **effective immediately**, with all the powers and duties of the Corporate Secretary, **for the 2026 annual meeting of the members** and until the Board elects a Corporate Secretary at the organizational meeting that follows the election of trustees, or until further action of the Board.

**2. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Secretary pro tempore shall (a) **send written notice** of the annual meeting to every member entitled to vote **by electronic mail and by [another manner]**, not later than ______ November 2026, with proof of sending; (b) collect the **waivers of notice**, if the annual meeting is to be held on 21 November 2026, or send the notice for **Saturday, 12 December 2026**, as the Board decides in paragraph 3; (c) receive the **nominations and proxies**; (d) prepare the **list of members** entitled to vote and certify the **quorum**; and (e) **keep the minutes and records** of the meeting.

**3. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the annual meeting of the members shall be held on **[Saturday, 21 November 2026 only if every member entitled to vote has signed a written waiver of notice by 12:00 noon on Friday, 20 November 2026; otherwise on Saturday, 12 December 2026]** **[or: Saturday, 12 December 2026]** at ______________________, Makati City, and the Secretary pro tempore shall issue notice accordingly.

**4. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the person who has been acting as Corporate Secretary, **______________________________**, shall **deliver to the Secretary pro tempore within two (2) days** the corporate records, minute books, membership list, proxies received and corporate seal, if any, against a signed inventory and receipt, **without prejudice** to the rights of that person as a member and trustee.

**5. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Secretary pro tempore shall keep the minutes, attendance sheets, waivers, proxies and ballots, shall report the election to the Securities and Exchange Commission, including the **General Information Sheet within thirty (30) days** after the election, and shall act at all times in compliance with the By-Laws, the Magna Carta and the COMELEC guidelines.

**6. RESOLVED FINALLY**, that this Resolution **takes effect immediately** upon approval, and that the Board shall hold its regular meetings as the By-Laws provide, or more frequently as the Board decides, with notice to all trustees.
