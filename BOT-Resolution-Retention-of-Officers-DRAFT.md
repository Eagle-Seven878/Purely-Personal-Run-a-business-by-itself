# BOARD RESOLUTION NO. ____, SERIES OF 2026

**A RESOLUTION ENDORSING THE RETENTION OF THE INCUMBENT NATIONAL PRESIDENT, EXECUTIVE NATIONAL VICE PRESIDENT, AND TRUSTEES JIMUEL EMPAS, ROY CARL BARRIENTOS, JOHN PHILIP ILANO, AND [FULL NAME] SANLADIN, AND AFFIRMING THE BY-LAWS-MANDATED ELECTION ON 21 NOVEMBER 2026**

---

**WHEREAS**, the Fraternal Order of Haribon Haring Ibon International Incorporated (the "Order") is governed by its Articles of Incorporation and By-Laws, which are the supreme internal rules of the organization and bind the Board of Trustees, the officers, and every member;

**WHEREAS**, Article ____, Section ____ of the By-Laws fixes the regular election of the Board of Trustees and national officers on the **third Saturday of November** of each year, which in 2026 falls on **Saturday, 21 November 2026**;

**WHEREAS**, the date prescribed by the By-Laws is a matter of rule, not of convenience, and any election held on a date other than that prescribed would be contrary to the By-Laws and open to challenge as invalid, to the prejudice of the Order and its members;

**WHEREAS**, the incumbent **National President**, **[FULL NAME]**, who also serves as Vice President of the Board of Trustees, and the incumbent **Executive National Vice President**, **[FULL NAME]**, have led the Order with dedication, discipline, and fidelity to the By-Laws, and have kept its operations, chapters, and programs moving forward;

**WHEREAS**, Trustees **Jimuel Empas, Roy Carl Barrientos, John Philip Ilano, and [FULL NAME] Sanladin** have likewise rendered faithful and active service to the Board and the Order;

**WHEREAS**, the continuity of leadership at this stage is vital to protect ongoing programs, preserve institutional knowledge, maintain good standing with regulators, and ensure an orderly, peaceful, and lawful conduct of the election;

**WHEREAS**, the Board of Trustees is empowered to adopt resolutions in the best interest of the Order and to recommend to the membership the course of action that will best secure its stability and growth;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, on motion duly made and seconded, the Board of Trustees, in a meeting duly called and with a quorum present, **RESOLVED, as it hereby RESOLVES**:

**1. Affirmation of Election Date.** The Board of Trustees affirms that the regular election shall be held on **Saturday, 21 November 2026**, the third Saturday of November, as required by the By-Laws, and that no other date shall be set or recognized for such election.

**2. Endorsement of Retention.** The Board of Trustees hereby **endorses and recommends to the General Membership the retention** of the following in their respective positions for the ensuing term:

   a. The incumbent **National President**, [FULL NAME];
   b. The incumbent **Executive National Vice President**, [FULL NAME];
   c. Trustee **Jimuel Empas**;
   d. Trustee **Roy Carl Barrientos**;
   e. Trustee **John Philip Ilano**; and
   f. Trustee **[FULL NAME] Sanladin**.

**3. Continuity of Service.** Pending the election and qualification of their successors, and in accordance with the By-Laws and applicable law, the above-named officers and trustees shall continue to discharge their duties and responsibilities so that no gap in the leadership and service of the Order shall occur.

**4. Rights of the Membership Preserved.** Nothing in this Resolution limits the right of any member in good standing to nominate, to be nominated, or to vote. The final choice of the Board of Trustees and officers rests with the members, who shall vote in accordance with the By-Laws on 21 November 2026.

**5. Other Trustees.** The other members of the Board of Trustees are invited to join in this Resolution by affixing their signatures below, signifying their concurrence. A trustee who declines to sign is not thereby prevented from participating in the election.

**6. Implementation.** The Corporate Secretary is directed to (a) furnish certified true copies of this Resolution to all chapters and members, (b) include it in the records of the Order, and (c) coordinate with the election committee to ensure the orderly conduct of the 21 November 2026 election.

**7. Effectivity.** This Resolution takes effect immediately upon its approval.
