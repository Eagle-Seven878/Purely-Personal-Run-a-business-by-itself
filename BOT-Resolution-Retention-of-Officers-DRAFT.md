## PART A: RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, the Order is a non-stock corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission on 15 April 2026 under Company Reg. No. 2026040246159-17, and governed by the **Revised Corporation Code of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 11232)**, its **Articles of Incorporation**, and its **By-Laws**;

**WHEREAS**, under Section V(b) of the By-Laws, the corporate powers of the Order are exercised, all its business conducted, and all its property controlled and held by the Board of Trustees;

**WHEREAS**, **Section II(a) of the By-Laws** provides that the regular or annual meeting of the members "shall be held annually on **3rd Saturday of November**", which in 2026 falls on **Saturday, 21 November 2026**, and the trustees are elected from among the members at the said annual meeting;

**WHEREAS**, **Section II(e) of the By-Laws** requires that meetings of members be held in the city or municipality where the principal office of the Order is located, which is **Makati City**;

**WHEREAS**, **Section II(b) of the By-Laws** requires written notice of the regular meeting to be sent to all members at least **twenty-one (21) days** before the meeting, and such notice may be sent by electronic mail;

**WHEREAS**, as of the date of this Resolution the written notice required by Section II(b) has **not** been sent to the members at least twenty-one (21) days before 21 November 2026, for the reason that ______________________________ [state the reason briefly and factually];

**WHEREAS**, the date and notice requirements fixed by the By-Laws bind the Board, the officers and the members alike, and an annual meeting and election held without compliance with them, unless every member entitled to vote waives notice in writing, would be **contrary to the By-Laws** and open to challenge, to the damage of the Order and its members;

**WHEREAS**, under **Section VI of the By-Laws**, immediately after their election the trustees must formally organize by electing a President, a Treasurer, a Secretary and such other officers as the By-Laws provide, and all officers hold office for one (1) year and until their successors are elected and qualified;

**WHEREAS**, under **Section V(a) of the By-Laws**, each trustee holds office until the successor is elected and qualified;

**WHEREAS**, the incumbent **National President**, **GILBERT HOLANDA TRANI**, and the incumbent **Executive National Vice President**, **ERICSON CARBALLO JOSON**, together with Trustees **JEMUEL REQUIROSO IMPAS, ROY GIL BARRIENTOS, JOHN PHILIP SANARES ILANO and DINLASAN CORPUZ REGINO**, have rendered faithful, diligent and continuous service to the Order since its organization;

**WHEREAS**, continuity of leadership and institutional knowledge is necessary to safeguard the programs, chapters, records and compliance obligations of the Order in its first year of registration, and to ensure an orderly, peaceful and lawful conduct of the election;

**WHEREAS**, the members of the Order entitled to vote are the members of the **National Assembly**, namely the **Governor, the Vice Governor and the three (3) National Assemblymen** elected in each region, who possess the qualifications set out in **[Article ___ of the Magna Carta]** and in the guidelines of the Order's COMELEC;

**WHEREAS**, the power to elect the trustees rests with those members, and the power to elect the officers rests with the trustees under Section VI, and the Board may recommend to them the course of action it considers to be in the best interest of the Order;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, on motion duly made and seconded, the Board of Trustees hereby **RESOLVES**, as it is hereby **RESOLVED**:

## PART B: SUBJECT I: DATE AND NOTICE OF THE ANNUAL MEETING AND ELECTION

**1. RESOLVED**, that the Board **affirms** that Section II(a) of the By-Laws fixes the annual meeting of the members on the **third Saturday of November**, that Section II(b) requires **at least twenty-one (21) days' written notice** to all members, that Section II(e) requires the meeting to be held in **Makati City**, and that all of these bind the Order.

**2. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that (a) the annual meeting and election **may proceed on Saturday, 21 November 2026** at ______________________, Makati City, **only if** every member entitled to vote has signed a **written waiver of notice** and a quorum of a majority of the members is present, the Corporate Secretary to collect and certify the waivers before the meeting is called to order; and (b) **if complete waivers are not obtained** by ______ (date/time), the Corporate Secretary shall send written notice to all members (by electronic mail or other manner allowed by the SEC) **not later than ______ November 2026** for the annual meeting and election to be held on **Saturday, 12 December 2026** (or on the earliest date that is at least twenty-one (21) days after the notice) at ______________________, Makati City, the incumbent trustees and officers **holding over** in the meantime under Sections V(a), V(b) and VI.

**3. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Corporate Secretary shall (a) confirm that written notice to all members complied with Section II(b) of the By-Laws, (b) receive proxies in the form required by Section IV of the By-Laws within a reasonable time before the meeting, (c) prepare the list of members entitled to vote and verify the **quorum of a majority of the members** required by Section III of the By-Laws, and (d) coordinate with the Election Committee, if any, for the orderly conduct of the election.

## PART C: SUBJECT II: ENDORSEMENT OF RETENTION

**4. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Board **endorses and recommends**:

   a. **To the members**, the re-election as trustees, for the ensuing term fixed by the By-Laws, of the following trustees: **JEMUEL REQUIROSO IMPAS, ROY GIL BARRIENTOS, JOHN PHILIP SANARES ILANO and DINLASAN CORPUZ REGINO**, and of the incumbent **National President, GILBERT HOLANDA TRANI**, and the incumbent **Executive National Vice President, ERICSON CARBALLO JOSON**; and

   b. **To the incoming Board of Trustees**, that immediately after the election, in the organizational meeting required by Section VI of the By-Laws, it **re-elect** the incumbent **National President** and the incumbent **Executive National Vice President** to their present offices, so that there is no gap in the leadership of the Order.

**5. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that pending the valid election and qualification of their successors, as provided in Sections V(a), V(b) and VI of the By-Laws, **all incumbent trustees and officers hold over**, and the above-named officers and trustees **shall continue to discharge their duties**, so that there is no gap in the leadership of the Order.

**6. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that any purported meeting of the members or election held **without compliance** with Sections II, III and IV of the By-Laws, or with the Magna Carta and the guidelines of the Election Committee (COMELEC) of the Order, is **not an act of the Order**; that the Corporate Secretary shall not canvass, certify, enter in the records or report to the Securities and Exchange Commission any result of such a meeting; and that the officers are authorized, on the advice of counsel, to cure any defect in notice (including by obtaining written waivers of notice from the members) and to take all lawful steps to protect the Order, its members and its records.

**7. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that this endorsement is a **recommendation only**. It does not limit the right of any member in good standing to nominate, to be nominated, to vote in person or by proxy, or to run for office, and the final choice rests with the members (as to the trustees) and with the incoming Board (as to the officers).

**8. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the trustees named or holding the offices described in paragraph 4 **have a personal interest in Subject II and disclose it**, and that Subject II is approved only if it receives the affirmative vote of a **majority of the trustees present** at a meeting at which a quorum is present, the votes of the interested trustees being recorded separately in the Record of Vote.

## PART D: GENERAL PROVISIONS

**9. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the other trustees may concur in this Resolution by signing below, and a trustee who declines to sign or who votes "Nay" shall not be prejudiced.

**10. RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Corporate Secretary is directed to (a) enter this Resolution in the Minutes and Corporate Records, (b) furnish copies to the members and chapters, and (c) keep the minutes, the list of members present or represented, the proxies and the results of the election, for the report on the election of trustees and officers and the **General Information Sheet to be filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission within thirty (30) days** from the election, as required by the Revised Corporation Code and SEC rules.

**11. RESOLVED FINALLY**, that the officers of the Order are authorized to do all acts and sign all documents necessary to carry out this Resolution, which **takes effect immediately** upon its approval.
