## Worksheet 2 · the 5-second test (was missing entirely)

v1 never states these. v2 adds them as the Offer Snapshot on page 1 of the Triangle Model:

- **Offer line (≤15 words, verb + outcome + ICP):** "I help earning owners keep 10% net income and pay themselves in 90 days." (14 words.) v1's headline was 22 words and had a typo ("game big sales").
- **Positioning (why you vs alternatives):** missing in v1. v2 adds a "Why not just keep doing what you're doing?" table: accountant, hype program, DIY spreadsheet, this method.
- **Exact price:** v1's "Php48K. Php50K. Then Php50K." never adds up the total. v2 shows ₱148,000.
- **90-day goal with a number:** 10% net income + fixed salary, for all six.
- **Daily key metric:** month-to-date net income against breakeven (the Morning Numbers). In v1 the Morning Numbers had no defined content.

## Open items for Gilbert to confirm before sending

1. **₱60,000/month testimonial:** a real client name and business, and permission. It is left as `[Client name], [business]` on purpose.
2. **BRO Lending board-seat story:** OK to reference publicly?
3. **Clarity Intensive: Personal Voice at ₱125,000:** is it really sold on its own at that price? If not, cut it or re-price it. A bonus worth close to the whole program makes buyers doubt the numbers (price-anchoring rule 5, "stack must not feel padded").
4. **Dates:** applications close Fri Oct 30, program starts Mon Nov 2, 2026. Change them if they don't fit his calendar.
5. **"The founding rate is for these six owners":** this is a true statement of fact, but his Voice DNA bans price-goes-up urgency. Keep or cut at his discretion.
6. **Apply link:** the button points to `#apply`. Swap in the real form URL.
7. **Stack multiple (lever 5):** to reach 5x without padding, add one real bonus he already sells, for example a done-with-you loan-readiness review. Don't invent a value to get there.
