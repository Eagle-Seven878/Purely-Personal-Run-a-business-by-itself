BLUE · THE PROJECTS, HOW WE DO IT

Numbers You Trust
  • The Breakeven Map. The monthly sales figure where you stop losing money, from your own books.
  • The Top Earners Rank. Every product or service ranked by what it actually earns you.
  • The Leak Stop. The first leak found and closed, the recovered cash counted.
Profit You Keep
  • The Profit Set-Aside. Profit moved to its own account the day money comes in.
  • The Peso Plan. Every peso of income given a job before it is spent.
  • The Weekly Money Check. A 10-minute weekly read that keeps the profit there in a slow month.
Pay You Take
  • The Fixed Salary. A real salary amount, paid on the same day every month.
  • The Morning Numbers. One daily message: sales, cash, and net income against breakeven.
  • The Numbers Navigator. Your own AI system that sends the Morning Numbers, yours to keep.
