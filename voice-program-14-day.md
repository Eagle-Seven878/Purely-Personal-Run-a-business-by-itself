# 14-Day Voice Program: Pace, Clarity, Confidence

10-15 minutes a day. You need a phone with a voice recorder and a timer. Optionally add a free transcription tool (Google Recorder, Otter, or the iPhone Voice Memos transcript).

**Safety:** hum gently to warm up first. Drink water. Stop if your throat hurts. Never push or strain.

---

## START TODAY: Day 1 Baseline (15 min)

1. Record **2 minutes** talking about something you know well (your day, your work, a hobby). Don't prepare.
2. Record **1 minute** reading the script below out loud.
3. Get the transcript, then fill in the Scorecard at the bottom:
   - **Words per minute (wpm):** word count ÷ minutes
   - **Fillers per minute:** count "um, uh, like, you know, so, basically" ÷ minutes
   - **Mis-heard words:** words the transcript got wrong (these are the words you mumble)
   - **Energy:** rate yourself 1-5
4. Save both recordings. You will compare them on Days 7 and 14.
5. Paste the transcript to Claude for a full analysis.

**Baseline reading script**
> The best way to improve how you speak is to listen to yourself. Most people are surprised by what they hear. They notice they speak too quickly, trail off at the end of sentences, or fill silence with extra words. The good news is that every one of these habits can be changed with a few minutes of focused practice each day. Start slowly, breathe, and let each word land before you move on to the next.

---

## Warm-up (use every day, 2 min)
- Gentle hum on "mmm", 10 seconds, 3 times
- Lip trills (like a motorboat), 10 seconds, 3 times
- Big yawn, then jaw circles, 5 times
- Say "ma-may-mee-mo-moo" slowly, 3 times

---

## Week 1: Foundation

**Day 2: Breathing**
- Breathe in through the nose for 4 counts. Breathe out on a steady "sss" for 10-15 counts. Repeat 5 times.
- Then say one full sentence per breath. Read 5 sentences from anything.
- Goal: finish sentences without running out of air.

**Day 3: Pace**
- Set a timer for 1 minute. Read the baseline script three times: slow (about 120 wpm), fast (about 170 wpm), then your target (about 145 wpm).
- Count words, check which pass felt natural, and note your target.

**Day 4: Clarity**
- Over-articulate slowly, then gradually speed up. Do each 3 times:
  - "Red leather, yellow leather"
  - "Unique New York"
  - "She sells seashells by the seashore"
  - "Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled peppers"
- Say each ending consonant (t, d, s, k) crisply.
- Re-read the Day 1 script and see if the transcript misses fewer words.

**Day 5: Pauses instead of fillers**
- Talk for 1 minute on any topic. Every time you feel an "um" coming, stay silent for 1 second instead.
- Record it, count fillers, and compare with Day 1.

**Day 6: Volume and projection**
- Read the script as if talking to someone across a large room, then to someone 3 feet away.
- Settle on the volume in between. Keep your chin level and shoulders relaxed.

**Day 7: Checkpoint**
- Re-record the same 2-minute topic and 1-minute script as Day 1.
- Fill in the Scorecard for Day 7. Rest your voice afterwards.

---

## Week 2: Delivery

**Day 8: Pitch variety**
- Read the script with exaggerated highs and lows (about 150% variation).
- Read it again at 70% of that. This is the natural sweet spot, which is lively but not theatrical.

**Day 9: Emphasis**
- Say "I never said she took my money" 7 times, stressing a different word each time. Notice how the meaning shifts.
- Pick the 2-3 key words in each sentence of the script and emphasize only those.

**Day 10: Strong endings**
- Statements should end on a falling pitch, not a rising one and not fading out.
- Say 10 statements, such as "I can help with that" or "The meeting is at three", and let each one land with a drop at the end.

**Day 11: Storytelling**
- Tell a 1-minute story with three parts: setup, turn, ending.
- Slow down for the key moment and pause before the punchline.

**Day 12: Real-world test**
- Record a real call, voice note, pitch or presentation (with consent where needed).
- Check pace, fillers and endings against your Day 1 baseline.

**Day 13: Fix your weak spot**
- Pick the weakest score on your Scorecard and repeat that day's drill twice.

**Day 14: Final test**
- Re-record the Day 1 recordings. Fill in the Scorecard and compare it with Day 1.
- Send the results to Claude for review and a next-month plan.
