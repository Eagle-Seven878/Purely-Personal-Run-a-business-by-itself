# IGNITE: what it is and who it's for

**Runtime:** ~45 seconds · ~150 spoken words (fill the bracketed slots with 1 short phrase each and it stays inside 40–50s)
**Platform:** Instagram Reels / TikTok / Shorts
**Voice:** Generic plain, warm spoken register (no Voice DNA used)
**Source:** Topic only. No facts about IGNITE were supplied, so every specific is a [bracketed slot] for you to fill in with the real thing.

---

## THE SCRIPT

### Hook

*(look straight at camera, easy and direct, like you're about to tell a friend something)*

If you're [who IGNITE is for, in the words they use about themselves], this video is for you.

I run a program called IGNITE, and I want to tell you what it actually is.

---

> ### ↕ Swap-in hooks — not part of the script, skip past to keep filming
>
> **A.** "Okay, I want to tell you about IGNITE, because a lot of you keep asking." — best if people are already DMing you about it.
>
> **B.** "IGNITE is for [who it's for], and here's what happens inside it." — best if you want it blunt and fast.

---

### Body

IGNITE is [one plain sentence on what it is: a program, an event, how long, where].

*(small beat, then a bit slower)*

And I built it because [the real reason you started it, in your own words].

So here's what happens. [The main thing people do or get in it.] And by the end, [the specific result people walk away with].

*(lean in a little, drop your voice)*

And honestly, the part people don't expect is [the one surprising or true thing about IGNITE only you'd know].

### Close

*(smile, back to your normal energy)*

So that's IGNITE.

If that sounds like you, follow me, because I'm going to be sharing [what they'll see next: dates, behind the scenes, stories from it], and you won't want to miss it.

---

## Why this hook

It does the two jobs in one second: it tells the right person this is for them, and it tells them what they're about to get, an explanation of IGNITE. A clever or mysterious opener would make people guess, and they'd scroll instead. Keep it plain.

## Filming notes

- I don't know what's inside IGNITE, so I didn't write any claims, numbers, names, or results. Anything that makes this video yours is in the brackets. A real specific, like a number, a moment, or a client line, will beat anything I could write.
- If you have a real result or a story from the program, put it in the "surprising thing" slot. That's the strongest beat in the script.
- If the filled-in version runs past 50 seconds, cut the "here's why I built it" line first.
- Pace: quick but relaxed, roughly 190–200 words a minute. The only real pauses are the two marked beats.
- On screen, if you have footage of the program, cut to it during the "here's what happens" line.
